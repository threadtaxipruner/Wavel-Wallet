What we do

Wavel is a non-custodial multichain wallet designed to remove friction between networks and give people one secure place for their assets, apps, and activity.

We bring supported networks together in one clear experience for sending, swapping, discovering apps, managing collectibles, and following activity. Security, transparency, and user control guide everything we ship.

We’re building the multichain wallet that should have always existed.

Secure & Transparent

Commitment to transparency

Wavel is open source and non-custodial. Your keys stay on your device, and transparency is foundational to everything we build.

Make self-custody everyday

Our users, their security, and our mission to make self-custody useful every day rise above all else. As part of Wavel, you’ll help put control directly in the hands of the people who use our wallet.

Our clients and their account security come first. We’re focused on advancing financial freedom and inclusion globally. We do this through education, entrepreneurship or grants to advance innovation in the crypto industry.

How we hire

Our recruiting process reflects our mission and values. Whether you’re applying for engineering, product, support, or anything in between, you’ll go through a structured, fully virtual process focused on skills, collaboration, and long-term impact.

We value what you’ve done, but we’re also looking at how you think, how quickly you learn, and how you push boundaries with resilience.

We also encourage all candidates to “dogfood” our products and dive deep into the world of crypto. Why? As stated in our culture document, “know thy self, know thy enemy, know thy customer.” Not a Wavel customer yet? Pick across our product line and start your crypto journey!

Here’s what you can expect:

Raise your hand

We start with a CV screen to ensure you meet the role requirements. Use this opportunity to show the work that best represents how you think, collaborate, and build.

Talent screen

You’ll meet with one of our talent partners for an initial conversation about role fit, mission alignment, motivation, and career ambitions.

Role specific interviews

Next comes interviews with your hiring manager, future teammates, and key stakeholders. This is where we explore your skills, experience, and how you operate in context.

Practical exercise

You’ll apply your critical thinking to a real-world case study that aligns with your role and helps us understand your approach to solving complex challenges.

Offer

Join us and help build the everyday multichain wallet!

Note: Our practical exercises can fall at different stages in our processes depending on the role. Your recruiter will walk you through what to expect.
